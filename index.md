# rclone-sirius

**rclone-sirius** is a personal OAuth client used by its owner to synchronize
their own Google Drive files with a personal Linux workstation (`sirius-b`)
using [rclone](https://rclone.org).

- **Purpose:** two-way sync of the owner's academic and research folders.
- **Users:** the owner only. This is not a public service.
- **Data handling:** see the [Privacy Policy](./privacy).

Maintained by [@octrodriguez](https://github.com/octrodriguez).
